## What was built

- `mcp/src/handlers.ts` (8 tools on the SDK), `auth.ts` (OAuth 2.1, stateless
  sealed tokens), `service.ts` (Streamable HTTP, stateless), `stdio.ts` (bin
  `senseplane-mcp`). ADR 0023.
- API: `WatchCreate.duration_minutes`, `Watch.expires_at`, status `expired`
  (migration 0007, swept every 30 s).
- Spec: tool descriptions tightened (`look` returns the image and, with a
  question, an answer; `listen` returns sound episodes, never speech).
- Docs: `docs/quickstart.md`, `mcp/README.md`, `.env.example`.

## Known limits at the time

- `listen` returns sound classes with confidence, no loudness levels.
- OAuth codes could be replayed within their 2-minute life; they are
  single-use since ADR 0026.
