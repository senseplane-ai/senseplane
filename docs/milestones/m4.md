  engine (objects enter/leave/present/stationary with zones and counts, motion
  per zone, sound, schedule, sequence, cooldown, active hours), VLM verification,
  ask/describe, local embeddings, usage events.
- API: `/v1/watches` CRUD (hand edits validated against the spec), sensor
  watches on readings, `POST /v1/sources/{id}/ask`, `/v1/memory/search`
