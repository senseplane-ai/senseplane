# Security policy

Senseplane handles video and audio from people's homes and businesses. We take
reports seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **security@senseplane.com** with a description, the affected version or
commit, and steps to reproduce. Please do not open a public issue.

- We acknowledge reports within 3 working days.
- We aim to ship a fix for critical issues within 14 days and will credit you
  in the release notes unless you ask us not to.
- Please give us reasonable time to fix the issue before public disclosure.

## Scope

Everything in this repository: the API, media plane, brain, MCP server, SDKs,
console, bridge and the self-host compose bundle.
