# 12. H.264 only for video in v1

Date: 2026-10-15 · Status: accepted

## Decision
v1 accepts H.264 video and Opus or AAC audio. H.265 and other codecs are
rejected at ingest with `unsupported_codec`, and the docs show how to switch a
camera to H.264. Nothing is transcoded. H.265 is revisited after v1.
